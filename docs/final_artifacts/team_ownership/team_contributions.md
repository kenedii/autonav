- Data collection was collaborative work by both Nicolas and Chris.

## Recommended presentation framing

- Present ownership by the subsystem each person is most prepared to explain.
- Avoid claiming exclusive authorship for heavily shared files.
- If asked about a mixed file, explain which part each person can confidently walk through and hand off where needed.
